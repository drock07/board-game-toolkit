Sealed bids, drafting, rock-paper-scissors, everyone hitting or standing
against the dealer: some rounds have every player act at the same time. Two
nodes do it: `everyone` when each player answers once, and `simultaneous`
for anything longer.

## Everyone answers once

`everyone(…actions)` waits for each player to take one of the actions, in
any order. The node ends when all have answered.
[Sealed Bids](/board-game-toolkit/examples/sealed-bids/) runs one per lot:

<Source file="games/sealed-bids/game.ts" region="round" />

While it waits, `actors()` lists everyone who hasn't answered yet, and
`view.waiting` has one entry per open prompt. Each player's legal inputs
are only their own. A player who has bid is done; the others can still bid,
in any order.

Several players take the same action here, so `execute` tells them apart by
its `actor` argument, the player who sent the input:

## Fibers

`everyone(…)` is shorthand for `simultaneous(prompt(…))`. `simultaneous(body)`
runs its body once for every player at the same time, each on its own
**fiber**: a separate stack of frames, bound to that player. Each fiber waits
on its own player and moves on its own, and `simultaneous` ends when every
fiber has finished.

The body can be any flow. Here everyone draws from one pile as often as
they like, then stops, each with their own `turn`:

<Source file="snippets/simultaneousTurns.ts" region="game" />

- **`simultaneous(turn(…))`** gives each player their own turn, with its own
  counts. One player stopping doesn't stop anyone else.
- **Inside a fiber, the bound actor is the fiber's player.** A `prompt`
  waits on them, `actor` in an action is them, and `s.actor` in a condition
  is them. So a `branch` on `s.actor` can let some players sit a round out.
- **The flow carries on after** the last fiber finishes.

The [simultaneous demo](/board-game-toolkit/reference/flow/simultaneous/)
is the same shape: everyone rolls toward a target at once.

## What each player sees

Fibers are private in what they show. A `turn` on bob's fiber shows its
counts to bob, in `turnNode.shown(view)`, and not to anyone else: a view's
`shown` holds the root flow's and the viewer's own fibers', never another
player's. What every player sees is who the game is waiting on,
`view.waiting`, because that's public at a real table too.

Choices are a different matter. Anything a player chooses that others
mustn't see until later belongs in a zone only they can see, not in vars.
Sealed Bids puts each bid in the bidder's own `"owner"` zone:

<Source file="games/sealed-bids/game.ts" region="box" />

The other players' views hold a placeholder: they can see that you've bid,
not how much. The round's last step reads every bid from the full state.
See [Hidden information](/board-game-toolkit/guides/hidden-information/).

## Reactions in a fiber

The draw game's trap is an [ability](/board-game-toolkit/concepts/effects/)
carried by a chip: when one enters your row, you must disarm it. It runs
**on the fiber that caused it**, so it holds up only the player who drew
it. While ann disarms her trap, bob keeps drawing.

Sometimes a reaction must stop the whole table: everyone waits while a
player resolves something that changes what the others may do. Give the
ability `pause: "everyone"`:

<Source file="snippets/simultaneousTurns.ts" region="held" />

While a `pause: "everyone"` ability runs, no other fiber moves or takes
input. `actors()` is just the player answering it. When it's done, every
fiber carries on where it was.

A player can have two prompts open at once: their own turn, and a reaction
on someone else's fiber that asks them. An input goes to the prompt that
offers its action.

## Variations

- **First answer wins**: [`anyone`](/board-game-toolkit/reference/flow/anyone/)
  waits for one answer from any of a set of players.
- **Reveal together**: keep choices hidden in owner zones while everyone
  chooses, then flip or move them to a public zone in the step after.
- **Ending early**: an [outcome](/board-game-toolkit/concepts/outcomes/)
  raised in one fiber cancels the others too, and carries on in the parent.
