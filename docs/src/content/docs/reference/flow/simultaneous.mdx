`simultaneous` is for rounds where everyone plays at once and nobody waits
for anybody: each player rolls until they stop, everyone drafts from their
own pack. You write `simultaneous(body)`.

| Argument | Type   | What it is                                            |
| -------- | ------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| `body`   | `Node` | What each player does. Any node, as deep as you like. |

## Demo

Both players roll toward 12 at the same time, each taking as many rolls as
they like before they stop. The round ends when both have stopped.

## Fibers

`simultaneous` starts one **fiber** per player, in seat order: a separate
stack of frames running the body, bound to that player. Inside a fiber:

- a `prompt` or `turn` waits on the fiber's player, and `s.actor` and
  `tx.actor` are that player;
- the fiber moves on its own: one player answering never waits for
  another, and their prompts are open at the same time;
- the fiber ends when its body does.

`simultaneous` ends when every fiber has ended, and the flow carries on
after it.

## Behavior

- **Waiting.** `view.waiting` has one entry per open prompt, in fiber
  order, so a UI can show who's still deciding.
- **Shown.** Each player sees what their own fiber's nodes show, on top of
  what the shared flow shows: in the demo, `turnNode.shown(view)` is your
  own turn's counts. See
  [What nodes show](/board-game-toolkit/reference/flow/shown/).
- **Outcomes.** An outcome raised in one fiber that no `outcomes` inside
  that fiber handles cancels every fiber, and unwinds on from the
  `simultaneous`. Guards on nodes outside it are checked as usual, and
  cancel all the fibers when they fire.
- **Reactions.** An ability set off in one fiber runs on that fiber, and
  holds only that player; the others keep going. An ability with
  `pause: "everyone"` holds every fiber while it runs. See
  [Effects and abilities](/board-game-toolkit/reference/flow/effects/).
- **Every player.** There's no option to choose the players. Branch on
  `s.actor` in the body to give some players nothing to do.

For one answer from each player, use the shorthand
[`everyone`](/board-game-toolkit/reference/flow/everyone/).
