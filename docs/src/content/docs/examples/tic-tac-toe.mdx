Two players alternate until someone makes a line or the board fills. The
[Quickstart](/board-game-toolkit/start/quickstart/) builds this game step by
step; this page covers what it doesn't.

## How it works

**The board is vars, not zones.** Nine cells don't move or hide, so they're
data. There are no entity types or zones at all.

<Source file="games/tic-tac-toe/game.ts" region="types" />

**A session loops forever.** Each pass of the outer `loop` is one game: a step
clears the board and draws a random starter, `turns` runs until the board is
decided, a step records the result, and a prompt labelled "Play again" waits.

<Source file="games/tic-tac-toe/game.ts" region="rules" />

- **`from: (s) => s.vars.starter`**: `turns` starts from the seat a step drew
  with `tx.random.pick`, then goes on in seat order. The draw is seeded, so a
  replay gets the same starter.
- **`until`**: checked before each turn, so the game stops right after the
  move that makes a line, not at the end of a round.
- **The last prompt**: `again` has no args and changes nothing. Answering it
  ends the `seq`, and the loop starts the next game.

**The computer is a bot.** A bot is a function from its legal inputs and its
own view to one of those inputs. This one scores every legal placement with
minimax and picks among the best with its own seeded `random`:

<Source file="games/tic-tac-toe/bots.ts" region="bot" />

The page gives the second seat to the bot as its `controller`. The host runs
it when its seat may act, after a short delay, and the engine checks its input
like anyone else's. See [Bots](/board-game-toolkit/guides/bots/).
