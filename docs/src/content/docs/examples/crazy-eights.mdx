Match the top card's color or number, or play an eight to call a new color.
The first to empty their hand wins.

## How it works

**Each zone declares who can see it.** Hands are per-player zones
(`hand:p1`, `hand:p2`, …) with `visibility: "owner"`, the deck is `"hidden"`,
and the discard pile is `"top"`: only its top card shows. Switch **Viewing as**
in the inspector to see another seat's view. Bots get their own view too, so
they can't cheat. See [Views and hidden information](../../concepts/views/).

**Playing an eight asks for a color.** The `playCard` action has a `then` flow:
a `branch` that, when an eight was played, runs a `choose` prompt over the
`colors` list. See [`choose`](../../reference/flow/choose/).
