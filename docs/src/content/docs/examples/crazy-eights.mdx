Match the top card's color or number, or play an eight to call a new color.
The first to empty their hand wins.

## How it works

**Each zone says who may see it.** The cards are this game's own entity type,
colored cards rather than the shared playing cards.

<Source file="games/crazy-eights/game.ts" region="box" />

- **`deck`** is `"hidden"`: no one sees its cards.
- **`discard`** is `"top"`: everyone sees its top card and nothing beneath.
  The engine turns the covered card face down itself, as a `flipped` event
  the page plays back.
- **`hand`** is a zone family with `perPlayer: true`, one per seat, read as
  `hand.of(p)`. `"owner"` visibility shows each to its owner only. Switch
  **Viewing as** in the inspector to see another seat's view.

See [Views](/board-game-toolkit/concepts/views/).

**The active color is a var.** It's public and doesn't move, so it lives in
vars. An eight's player sets it; otherwise playing a card sets it to that
card's color.

**Playing an eight asks for a color.** Each turn is a prompt wrapped in
`outcomes`. `playCard` returns `{ exit: "wild" }` when the card it played is
an eight, and the `wild` outcome's `then` prompts the same player, still
bound by `turns`, to call a color:

<Source file="games/crazy-eights/game.ts" region="turns" />

<Source file="games/crazy-eights/game.ts" region="actions" />

- **`drawCard`** and **`pass`** are legal only when you can't play, so
  `legalInputs` never offers a draw you aren't allowed to take.
- **`drawCard`** reshuffles the discards, keeping the top card, when the deck
  runs out.

**The bots see what you see.** The two other seats are played by
`simpleBot`. A bot gets the same legal inputs and its own view, so it sees
only its own hand. See [Bots](/board-game-toolkit/guides/bots/).
