Play cards with your energy to attack and block, then take the enemy's hit.
Bring its health to zero before it does the same to you.

<Example slug="tower-battler" />

## How it works

**The fight ends the moment someone falls.** The `rounds` loop's exits are
checked after every transaction, so a card that deals the last point of damage
ends the loop at once, mid-turn. Both use inline expressions on vars. See
[Outcomes and guards](/board-game-toolkit/concepts/outcomes/).

<Source file="games/tower-battler/spec.ts" />

**Cards are data.** Each card's props list its effects, and playing one
resolves them all in the same transaction. Every change becomes an event, in
order, so the page plays them back as damage, block and draws. See
[Card effects](/board-game-toolkit/guides/card-effects/).

<Source file="games/tower-battler/impl.ts" region="playCard" />

**The enemy's attack emits a custom event.** `tx.emit` adds a presentation-only
event that changes no state. The page flashes the damage when it plays back.
See [Animating events](/board-game-toolkit/guides/animating-events/).
