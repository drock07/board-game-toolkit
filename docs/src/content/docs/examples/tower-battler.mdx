Play cards with your energy to attack and block, then take the enemy's hit.
Bring its health to zero before it does the same to you.

## How it works

**Cards are entities; the fighters are vars.** The draw pile is hidden, and
the hand and discard pile are public. Health, block, energy and the enemy's
next attack are numbers in vars.

<Source file="games/tower-battler/game.ts" region="types" />

**Cards are data.** Each card's props list what it does, in order. Playing
one resolves all of it in the same transaction:

<Source file="games/tower-battler/game.ts" region="playCard" />

Every change is an event, in order, so the page plays a card back as its
damage, block and draws. Drawing from an empty draw pile shuffles the
discards back in first, inside the same transaction.

**Your turn stays open.** It's a `turn` node: you play as many cards as your
energy allows, and `endTurn` returns `"end"`. Then a step discards your hand
and the enemy attacks.

**The fight ends the moment someone falls.** The fight is wrapped in
`outcomes` whose guards are checked after every transaction:

<Source file="games/tower-battler/game.ts" region="fight" />

So a Strike that deals the last point of damage ends the fight mid-turn, and
the enemy never attacks. See
[Outcomes](/board-game-toolkit/concepts/outcomes/).

**The enemy's attack is an effect.** A step works out how much your block
stops and causes `enemyAttack` with the result. The effect's `resolve`
applies the damage:

<Source file="games/tower-battler/game.ts" region="enemyAttack" />

Causing it is also an `effect` event, with the data typed. The page listens
with `useGameEffect` and flashes the hit, holding playback until the
animation is done:

<Source file="games/tower-battler/TowerBattler.tsx" region="hit" />

See [Effects and abilities](/board-game-toolkit/concepts/effects/) and
[Animating events](/board-game-toolkit/guides/animating-events/).
