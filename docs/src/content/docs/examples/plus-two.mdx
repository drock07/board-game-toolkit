A small Uno-style game whose +2 cards stack. Play a +2 and anyone else may
stack another on it, passing the growing penalty on, or the victim takes it.

<Example slug="plus-two" />

## How it works

**A trigger opens the response window.** The `plusTwo` trigger watches for a
card moving from a hand to the discard pile. When its `when` condition holds,
it interrupts whatever made the move, even another window, and runs its flow.
See [Triggers](/board-game-toolkit/reference/spec/triggers/) and
[Reactions and interrupts](/board-game-toolkit/guides/reactions/).

**The first answer wins.** The window is a `parallel` with `join: "race"`: the
other players get a `stack` prompt and the victim gets a `takePenalty` prompt,
all at once. The first answer ends the race and cancels the other branch. See
[`parallel`](/board-game-toolkit/reference/flow/parallel/).
