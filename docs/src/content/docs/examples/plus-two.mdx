A small Uno-style game whose +2 cards stack. Play a +2 and anyone else may
stack another on it, passing the growing penalty on, or the victim takes it.

<Example slug="plus-two" />

## How it works

**The penalty is vars.** Hands are per-player zones with `"owner"`
visibility. Who stacked last, who the penalty targets and how big it is are
public, so they're vars.

<Source file="games/plus-two/game.ts" region="types" />

**Each turn is a prompt, then the window.** Playing a card that's a +2 adds
to the penalty and names the next seat as the victim. After the prompt, the
turn runs the penalty window:

<Source file="games/plus-two/game.ts" region="rules" />

**The window is an `anyone` in a loop.**

<Source file="games/plus-two/game.ts" region="window" />

- **`anyone`** waits for the first answer from any player `who` names, here
  everyone but the newest stacker. The first answer ends it.
- **`loop({ until })`** runs while a penalty is pending. When there's none,
  the loop ends before the first pass, so a turn without a +2 has no window.
  A stack goes round again with a bigger penalty, and the turn continues once
  someone takes it.

**Each action's legality decides who may do what.** `anyone` offers both
actions to everyone it waits on. `stackPlusTwo` is legal only with a +2 in
hand, and `accept` only for the victim:

<Source file="games/plus-two/game.ts" region="accept" />

**The window doesn't give hands away.** `actors()` and `view.waiting` are
public. The window waits on every player but the stacker, whether they hold a
+2 or not, so who it waits on says nothing about anyone's hand. See
[Hidden information](/board-game-toolkit/guides/hidden-information/).
