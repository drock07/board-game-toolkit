## How it works

**Two public hands and a hidden shoe.** The dealer's zone is public, like
the player's; the hole card is hidden another way.

<Source file="games/blackjack/game.ts" region="types" />

**The hole card is face down in a public zone.** Each card is dealt to the
bottom of a hand, so hands read in deal order, and the dealer's second card
is dealt with `faceUp: false`. That override hides one entity, whatever its
zone's visibility:

<Source file="games/blackjack/game.ts" region="deal" />

Turning it up later is `tx.flip`, which is a `flipped` event. Every view shows
the card from then on. See [Views](/board-game-toolkit/concepts/views/).

<Source file="games/blackjack/game.ts" region="reveal" />

**Guards end play early.** Your turn and the dealer's are wrapped in
`outcomes` with two guards:

<Source file="games/blackjack/game.ts" region="play" />

- **`natural`**: holds right after the deal when you have 21 in two cards, so
  play is skipped before you're asked anything.
- **`bust`**: guards are checked after every transaction, so the `hit` that
  takes you past 21 cancels the turn at once, and the dealer never draws.
- Neither outcome has a `then`. The `outcomes` node just ends, and the flow
  carries on to settle the hand.

See [Outcomes](/board-game-toolkit/concepts/outcomes/).

**Your turn is a `turn` node.** A `prompt` ends after one answer; a `turn`
holds "Hit or stand" open across as many hits as you like. It ends when an
action returns `"end"`, as `stand` does, or when its `until` holds, here a
total of 21.

<Source file="games/blackjack/game.ts" region="actions" />

`placeBet` enumerates chip amounts and all-in, not every amount: `enumerate`
may be partial, and `validate` still accepts any whole amount in range. See
[`turn`](/board-game-toolkit/reference/flow/turn/).

**A branch handles going broke.** After settling, the first case whose
condition holds runs; otherwise the last argument does:

<Source file="games/blackjack/game.ts" region="broke" />

**The page deals one card at a time.** It waits a moment on each `moved` and
`flipped` event as it plays back, so the hole card gets a beat before it
turns:
